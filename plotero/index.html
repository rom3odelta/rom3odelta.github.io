Privacy Policy — Plotero

Effective date: 2026-08-18
App: Plotero (Android)
Operator: Hyper Koala Studio
Contact: hyperkoalastudio@gmail.com

What this app is

Plotero draws the shape of a land lot from the bearings and distances written in a Philippine land title's technical description. You type or paste that description, and the app plots the boundary, closes it, and reports the area. It is a drawing and checking tool for landowners and brokers — it is not a survey instrument, and it does not produce an official survey document.

Data we collect

None. Plotero has no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours behind it. We do not receive anything you enter, and we hold no copy of any lot you draw.

Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account identifier — there is no account to make.
  • Your location. The app has no location permission and never asks for a GPS fix.
  • Your contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, photos, or any other personal data on your device.
  • Advertising identifiers. There is no advertising in the app.
  • Usage analytics or crash reports of our own.

What the app stores on your device

Everything you enter stays on your phone, in the app's own private storage. A saved lot contains only:

  • the bearings and distances you entered — the technical description of the boundary;
  • the tie line and the designation of the monument it starts from, for example BLLM No. 1;
  • an optional nickname you type yourself, and the date you saved the lot.

That is the whole record. Plotero deliberately does not keep the registered owner's name, the title number, the plan number, or the municipality: those are stripped out when a title is read, and the original text of a title is never saved. What remains is a shape and its measurements, which on its own does not identify a person or a parcel.

What the app sends

Your lots never leave your phone. No version of Plotero uploads, backs up, syncs, or shares the bearings, distances, nicknames, or drawings you create. The app works completely with the phone in airplane mode.

The only request the app can ever make is a check for a small file that says whether a newer version of Plotero exists — and only in builds where that check is switched on. That request carries no information about you and nothing about your lots. Like any request to a website, it reveals your device's IP address and which version you are running; we store nothing about it. Builds with the check switched off make no network requests whatsoever.

Third parties

Plotero contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking libraries. Nothing in the app sends data to another company.

How to delete your data

Your lots live on your device for as long as you keep them there:

  • Delete one lot — open Saved and use the delete action on that lot.
  • Delete everything — uninstall Plotero. Android removes the app's private storage, and every saved lot goes with it.

Because the data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, and no copy of it for us to hand to anyone.

Children

Plotero is a property tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Plotero ever sends anything off the device — for example, if reading a photographed title comes to rely on an online service — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will say what is being sent. The "Effective date" at the top refers to the current version of this policy.
