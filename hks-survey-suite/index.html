HKS Survey Suite Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-12
App: HKS Survey Suite (Android)
Operator: Hyper Koala Studio
Contact: privacy@hyperkoalastudio.com

What this app is

HKS Survey Suite draws a land lot from the bearings and distances in a technical description, and lets you add lines, labels and notes on top. It is a drawing and checking tool. It is not a survey instrument, and it does not produce an official survey plan.

In short

We collect nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics. What you type and draw stays on your phone, and the app sends none of it to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

Your projects are saved in the app's own storage on your device. A project holds its name, the date you last changed it, and what you drew in it: lots with their bearings and distances, lines, polylines and labels. Two settings are saved the same way: whether labels are shown, and which project you had open last.

None of it leaves the phone. Deleting a project deletes it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes everything it stored.

What the app sends

Nothing. This version does not contact our servers or any other service while you use it. It does not ask for your location, camera, contacts or files.

Google Play installs and updates the app, and Google's own privacy policy covers what Play records when it does.

Children

The app is meant for users aged 13 and over, including students. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

Later versions may add optional features that do send data, such as signing in with Google, backing up to your own Google Drive, or in-app purchases. This page will be updated to describe each one before the version that has it is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@hyperkoalastudio.com. App support: support-hkssurveysuite@hyperkoalastudio.com.
