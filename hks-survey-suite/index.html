HKS Survey Suite Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16
App: HKS Survey Suite (Android)
Operator: Hyper Koala Studio
Contact: privacy@hyperkoalastudio.com

What this app is

HKS Survey Suite draws a land lot from the bearings and distances in a technical description, and lets you add lines, labels and notes on top. It is a drawing and checking tool. It is not a survey instrument, and it does not produce an official survey plan.

In short

The app works without an account. If you never sign in or buy anything, what you type and draw stays on your phone and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Signing in with Google is optional and only used to back up your projects to your own Google Drive. There are no ads and no analytics.

What the app stores on your phone

Your projects are saved in the app's own storage on your device. A project holds its name, the date you last changed it, and what you drew in it: lots with their bearings and distances, lines, polylines and labels. A few settings are saved the same way: whether labels are shown, which project you had open last, when you last backed up, and your credit balance with a short history of credits added and used.

Deleting a project deletes it from the phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes everything it stored on the phone.

Signing in with Google (optional)

If you tap Sign in with Google, Google shows you which account the app will use and asks for permission. The app receives your Google account's email address, which it shows on the More screen so you know which account you're using. It does not send your email address to us.

The only Google data permission the app asks for is the hidden app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). The app can't see or open any other file in your Drive.

Backup to your Google Drive

When you tap Back up now, the app copies your projects into that hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. When you tap Restore, it reads them back. The backup sits in your Drive account. We never receive a copy and can't read it. Google's privacy policy covers how Google stores files in Drive.

Purchases

Credits are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases with Google Play. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app, the details of what you bought and when, and basic device information such as the Android and app version. It does not receive your email address or your projects.

Deleting your data

On the More screen, Delete account removes every backup file the app made in your Google Drive and disconnects the app from your Google account. Projects on your phone are not touched by this; delete them in the app or clear the app's data to remove them. You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

For purchase records, email privacy@hyperkoalastudio.com.

What the app does not do

It doesn't show ads or run analytics, and it doesn't ask for your location, camera or contacts. It doesn't send your projects to our servers.

Google Play installs and updates the app, and Google's own privacy policy covers what Play records when it does.

Children

The app is meant for users aged 13 and over, including students. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

When a new version changes what the app collects or sends, this page is updated before that version is released, and the effective date above changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@hyperkoalastudio.com. App support: support-hkssurveysuite@hyperkoalastudio.com.
